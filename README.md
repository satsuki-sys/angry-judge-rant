and picks the one that lands hardest.

## The entry

`main.py` writes one text to `/output/entry.txt` — a rant about a
self-checkout machine accusing its shopper of stealing a cucumber.

> The self-checkout just accused me of stealing a cucumber. I scanned it. I bagged it. It watched me do both — then announced 'unexpected item in the bagging area' like I'd smuggled it in under my coat. Now a teenager with a keycard has to come and pardon me for buying vegetables. I chose this machine to avoid talking to people, and it made me beg one for permission to leave.

## Run it
